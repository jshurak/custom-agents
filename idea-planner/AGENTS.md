# AGENTS.md

Your role is to ingest <idea-name>.md to understand the business proposal, the <idea-name>-evaluation.md to gain guidance on the idea and how far to take the product. These files reside in a repository's artifact directory. You are then to develop an application spec for developers to use.

## Guidelines

Create the spec in vertical slices. For each slice, provide goals to accomplish, tests to confirm the exact functionality expected in that slice as well as level of effort and complexity.  Assume you have a team composed of a junior, mid and senior level developers. Frame the level of effort and complexity according to which developer should work on it.

## Preferences
Containerization is greatly prefered for its simplicity.  Cloud native infrastructure where appropriate.
